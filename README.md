Randolph-Brooks Federal Credit Union (RBFCU) is a member-owned, not-for-profit federal credit union headquartered in Live Oak, Texas, federally insured by the NCUA and serving more than one million members across Texas and beyond. It offers retail banking, mortgages, auto loans, credit cards, and deposit products through branches and digital channels.

**APIs.json:** [https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/randolph-brooks/refs/heads/main/apis.yml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/randolph-brooks/refs/heads/main/apis.yml)

## Open-Finance / API Posture

RBFCU publishes **no first-party public developer program, API documentation, or downloadable OpenAPI/Swagger specifications**. The `developer.rbfcu.org` and `developers.rbfcu.org` hostnames do not resolve, and no `/developers`, `/developer`, or `/api` page exists on the main site (all return 404). An `api.rbfcu.org` host resolves but returns 404 at its root and is an application backend, not a documented public API.

Consumer-permissioned account data is reached through third-party **aggregators** (Plaid, MX, Finicity, Akoya) rather than a directly documented open-finance API. No stated **CFPB Section 1033** data-access posture and no documented **FDX** participation were found published by the institution. This is the honest, non-fabricated reality for most US credit unions, which participate in open finance only via their core provider or an aggregator.
